About

Set the goal, do the repetitions, look honestly at the result. It works on court, and it works in code.

I grew up in Nicosia playing badminton at a level that didn’t leave much time for anything else — and still finished school with 19.2 out of 20. In 2025 I moved to Stockholm to study Computer Science and Engineering at KTH.

My first year covered the fundamentals: algorithms and data structures in C, digital design, embedded electronics and discrete mathematics. The rest of my time went into turning ideas into products with friends — one of them now takes real bookings and real payments every week.
