SSSB Market

A second-hand furniture board for students in Stockholm.

  • No accounts: each listing gets a secret 24-byte manage token that becomes the seller’s private edit link.
  • Built to stay clean: 5 posts per IP per hour, a honeypot for bots, and listings that expire after 60 days.
  • Fast on a phone: photos resized to 1280 px in the browser before upload; English and Swedish throughout.
