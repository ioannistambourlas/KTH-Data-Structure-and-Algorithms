Things I’ve built

01

Futsalaki

Matchmaking and pitch booking for futsal in Cyprus

A team says when it wants to play and at what level. Futsalaki finds an opponent with an overlapping time window, reserves a free pitch and takes payment — no group chats, no phoning around. Players use the iOS and Android app; venues run everything from a web dashboard with a calendar per pitch, the matchmaking pool, walk-in bookings, 7-a-side pitch combinations, revenue and payouts.

I co-founded the company with Panayiotis Charalambous. On the engineering side I focus on money and reliability: the Stripe Connect flow (payments, venue payouts, automatic refunds), database security and performance, rate limiting, monitoring and the CI pipeline.

02

SSSB Market

Second-hand furniture between students in Stockholm

Students in SSSB housing move in every August and out every June, and most of the furniture ends up in the bin room. SSSB Market lets them sell it to each other instead, starting with Lappis. Posting takes a minute and needs no account: photos, a price and a WhatsApp number.

Sellers get a private link to edit or mark an item sold, listings expire after 60 days, photos are compressed on the phone before upload, and posting is rate-limited. English and Swedish, with a small moderation panel. Built with Antreas Patsiantos.
